The Problem

Emergency response systems are often underprepared, fragmented, or inaccessible during crises. In areas with limited connectivity, the lack of reliable communication can leave people without timely information and support. Sentinel addresses this gap by providing accessible, location-based emergency information using NASA Earth observation data and local community input.

Our Approach

Sentinel combines NASA Earth observation data, predictive modeling, and crowdsourced community reporting to provide location-specific emergency information.

Our approach is to:

1. Collect NASA Earth observation data related to weather, land use, and disaster hotspots.
2. Process the data using predictive algorithms to forecast risks and identify unsafe zones.
3. Integrate crowdsourced user reports for real-time situational awareness.
4. Provide offline functionality so critical alerts remain accessible when connectivity is disrupted.
5. Deliver tailored guidance such as safe routes, nearby shelters, evacuation information, and critical safety instructions.

- Data sources: NASA Earth observation datasets
- Resources: Open-source disaster prediction algorithms, GIS and mapping tools
- Platform: Mobile application with offline-first functionality
- Team roles: Project Lead, Backend Developer, Frontend Developer, Data Scientist / GIS Specialist, UI/UX Designer, Communications Lead

Our Solution

Sentinel is a life-saving mobile application designed to adapt to different emergency situations. It uses NASA Earth observation data together with local community reports to provide real-time alerts, risk mapping, safe routes, nearby shelters, and actionable guidance based on the user's location.

The application is designed to support different scenarios, including conflict zones, natural disasters, and urban emergencies. Its offline-first design allows critical information to remain accessible even when communication infrastructure is disrupted.

Value Proposition

Sentinel combines global satellite intelligence with hyper-local community input. Unlike single-purpose emergency applications, it is designed to be versatile and scalable across different types of crises while focusing on helping people make faster and safer decisions.

Workflow
